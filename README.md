## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
1.We propose "AgriDirect", a centralized, AI-driven Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) and Business-to-Business (B2B) digital marketplace that seamlessly bridges the gap between farmers, Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs), bulk buyers (such as retailers and restaurants), and everyday consumers.The platform operates through a multi-tier interface:Mobile & Web App for Farmers/FPOs: Allows farmers to list their produce, set transparent pricing, indicate harvest timelines, and manage incoming orders with multi-lingual support.Smart Consumer Marketplace: A streamlined storefront for retail customers and bulk institutional buyers to purchase fresh produce directly at fair market rates.

2. How it addresses the problem

   Eliminating Intermediaries: By connecting producers directly with buyers, we bypass traditional multi-layered supply chains (local traders, commission agents, wholesalers), ensuring farmers capture a higher profit margin.Dynamic Fair Pricing: Transparent price discovery tools empower farmers to price their crops competitively based on real-time market trends while still undercutting inflated retail prices for consumers.Demand-Driven Farming: Providing farmers with localized demand forecasts helps them cultivate crops that match active market needs, reducing post-harvest surplus and distress sales.
3. Innovation and uniqueness of the solution

   Predictive Demand Forecasting: Utilizes machine learning models trained on historical consumption data, seasonal trends, and weather patterns to predict regional demand shifts and optimize crop cultivation schedules.AI-Powered Route & Fleet Optimization: Implements advanced spatial routing algorithms (such as genetic algorithms or vehicle routing optimization) to consolidate smallholder harvests, minimize fuel consumption, and reduce transit times for perishables.

1. Technologies to be used (e.g. programming languages, frameworks, hardware)

Data Ingestion & Preprocessing: Collect historical crop yields, local mandi pricing, weather data, and consumer consumption patterns.

Model Training: Train regression and time-series forecasting models (such as ARIMA or LSTM) to predict regional demand shifts.

Core Marketplace Development: Build user authentication, FPO inventory management, and direct order-matching workflows.

## Feasibility and Viability
Remove These Lines
1. Analysis of the feasibility of the idea
   High technical feasibility due to open-source mapping tools (OSM/OpenRouteService) and pre-trained machine learning frameworks.Strong socio-economic viability as digital smartphone penetration among Indian farmers and FPOs has grown exponentially, lowering adoption barriers.
2. Potential challenges and risks
   Low digital literacy among smallholder farmers in remote areas.Resistance from traditional local middlemen or commission agents.Perishable supply chain bottlenecks during peak summer months.
3. Strategies for overcoming these challenges
Partnering with local FPOs, NGOs, and Krishi Vigyan Kendras (KVKs) to conduct on-ground training workshops and provide simple vernacular voice-assisted UI options.
Offering immediate digital payment settlements and better margins to incentivize farmers to transition to the platform.
